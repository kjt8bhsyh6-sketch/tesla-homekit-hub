Apple Home gear categories

Home Hubs

A home hub runs automations while you're away, enables remote control and is required for Matter and Thread devices to work their best.

Garage & EV Charging

Make the garage the heart of your Tesla routine: it opens on arrival, closes when you leave, and you can charge overnight.

Smart Locks

Locks that support Apple Home Key let you tap an iPhone or Apple Watch to unlock, much like Phone Key on your Tesla. New: Apple Watch as your Tesla key.

Climate

Smart climate gear keeps the house comfortable and works alongside Tesla preconditioning. New: Scheduled Precondition for winter mornings and Cabin Overheat Protection.

Plugs & Power

Smart plugs turn lamps, fans and small appliances into Home accessories, and some measure energy use.

Sensors & Safety

Sensors trigger automations without anyone tapping anything: motion turns on lights, contacts report doors, leak sensors raise the alarm.

Cameras

HomeKit Secure Video cameras analyze footage on your home hub and store it end-to-end encrypted in iCloud. New: Sentry Mode at home and saving and backing up Dashcam clips.

Cameras: Apple Home guide

⬇ Download free PDF: HomeKit Secure Video Camera Guide

HomeKit Secure Video cameras analyze footage on your home hub and store it end-to-end encrypted in iCloud.

How to choose

  • You need iCloud+: the 50 GB plan covers 1 camera, 200 GB covers up to 5, 2 TB or more is unlimited.
  • Recordings cover the last 10 days and don't count toward iCloud storage.
  • A home hub (HomePod or Apple TV 4K) is required.

Apple Home setup, step by step

  1. In the Home app, tap the Add (+) button, then tap Add Accessory.
  2. Scan the QR code or enter the 8-digit Apple Home code (Matter devices use an 11- or 21-digit code), or hold your iPhone near the accessory.
  3. If the device was already paired to another Matter app, tap More options and select it.
  4. When asked to add the accessory to your network, tap Allow. Assign a room and a name (Siri uses this name), then tap Continue and Done.
  5. Tap the camera tile > Settings > Recording Options for When Home and When Away > Stream & Allow Recording.
  6. Turn on Activity Notifications: Home Settings > Cameras & Doorbells.

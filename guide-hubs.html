Home Hubs: Apple Home guide

⬇ Download free PDF: HomeKit Home Hub Setup Guide

A home hub runs automations while you're away, enables remote control and is required for Matter and Thread devices to work their best.

How to choose

  • Pick a HomePod mini or Apple TV 4K: both are Apple home hubs and Thread border routers.
  • Already use Hue lights? The Hue Bridge brings them into Apple Home.
  • For Aqara Zigbee sensors, the Aqara Hub M3 bridges them over Matter.

Apple Home setup, step by step

  1. Set up the HomePod mini or Apple TV with the same Apple Account you use for the Home app; it becomes a home hub automatically.
  2. On Apple TV: Settings > AirPlay and Apple Home > Room, to assign it to a room.
  3. Check hub status: Home app > Add (+)/More > Home Settings > Home Hubs & Bridges.
  4. Then add bridges (Hue Bridge, Aqara M3) with Add Accessory.

Tesla tie-ins

  • Without a hub, Tesla-arrival automations in the Home app won't run. Set up a hub first.
  • A HomePod in the garage lets you say "Hey Siri, open the garage" as you unload.

Tesla + Alexa: connect your Tesla to your smart home

⬇ Download free PDF: Tesla + Alexa Guide

Tesla doesn't offer an official Alexa skill today. The old "My Tesla" skill is reported dead, so voice control of the car from Echo devices goes through third-party services.

Can Alexa (instead of Siri) warm up my Tesla?

Yes, with a workaround. Tesla has no official Alexa skill, but third-party Tesla apps such as Tessie offer an Alexa skill. Link your Tesla account in the app, enable its skill in the Alexa app, then say something like “Alexa, ask Tessie to warm up my car” (climate on / precondition). It’s a paid third-party service, so check current pricing, and it’s not made by Tesla or Amazon. See the preconditioning by voice routine below for step-by-step setup.

Ways to connect

Tessie Alexa skill (most direct)

Enable the Tessie skill (US, UK, Canada, Australia) and link your Tessie account, then say "Alexa, ask Tessie Control to start climate". Tessie is a paid app: Tessie $6.99/mo, Tessie Pro $12.99/mo, with a free trial. Tessie warns that some skills may not work on Alexa+ yet.

Home Assistant

Home Assistant's Tesla Fleet integration can expose the car to Alexa through Home Assistant's Alexa support. Powerful, but a technical setup.

Tesla app on your phone

The Tesla app itself has no Alexa link, so keep using it (or Siri Shortcuts on iPhone) for anything a skill doesn't cover.

Example routines, step by step

1. Preconditioning by voice

  1. Enable the Tessie skill in the Alexa app and link your account.
  2. Alexa app > More > Routines > + > When This Happens > Voice: "Get the car ready".
  3. Add Action > Custom: "ask tessie control to start climate".
  4. Choose the Echo device and save. Say "Alexa, get the car ready".

2. Charge-status announcement

  1. New routine > When This Happens > Schedule: 9:00 PM daily.
  2. Add Action > Custom: "ask tessie control to get charge level" (Tessie lists "get charge level" as a command).
  3. Choose the kitchen Echo so it announces the result.

3. Garage on arrival (with limits)

  1. Link your garage opener's skill (for example Meross) and enable "Open" in the Alexa device settings with a 4-digit voice PIN.
  2. Alexa asks for the PIN every time you open by voice, so a fully automatic arrival open isn't supported. Use the Tesla car's HomeLink/Smart Garage (if available) or the opener's own app geofence.
  3. Closing usually works without a PIN, so an evening "Close the garage" routine is easy.

Compatible Matter gear

  • Echo (4th gen), Echo Hub and some Echo Show models act as Matter controllers. Check Amazon's device page for Matter and Thread support.
  • Matter plugs like Kasa KP125M and Tapo P125M pair to Alexa and Apple Home at the same time (Matter multi-admin).

Plugs & Power: Apple Home guide

⬇ Download free PDF: HomeKit Smart Plug Setup Guide

Smart plugs turn lamps, fans and small appliances into Home accessories, and some measure energy use.

How to choose

  • Matter-over-Thread plugs (Eve Energy) respond fast and strengthen your Thread network.
  • Wi-Fi Matter plugs (Tapo P125M) are budget-friendly.
  • Check the load rating. Never use a smart plug for an EV charger or space heater.

Apple Home setup, step by step

  1. In the Home app, tap the Add (+) button, then tap Add Accessory.
  2. Scan the QR code or enter the 8-digit Apple Home code (Matter devices use an 11- or 21-digit code), or hold your iPhone near the accessory.
  3. If the device was already paired to another Matter app, tap More options and select it.
  4. When asked to add the accessory to your network, tap Allow. Assign a room and a name (Siri uses this name), then tap Continue and Done.
  5. Use plugs in scenes and automations, for example lamps on at sunset.

Tesla tie-ins

  • A garage lamp on a smart plug can be part of your Arrive Home shortcut.
  • Keep EV charging on a dedicated, properly installed circuit.

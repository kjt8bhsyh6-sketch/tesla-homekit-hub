Garage & EV Charging: Apple Home guide

Make the garage the heart of your Tesla routine: it opens on arrival, closes when you leave, and you can charge overnight.

How to choose

  • Choose a HomeKit garage controller that fits your opener. Meross supports most openers with wired wall buttons.
  • Add a contact sensor (Eve Door & Window) if you want independent open/closed state.
  • For charging, Tesla's Wall Connector (NACS, up to 48 A) needs a qualified electrician.

Apple Home setup, step by step

  1. In the Home app, tap the Add (+) button, then tap Add Accessory.
  2. Scan the QR code or enter the 8-digit Apple Home code (Matter devices use an 11- or 21-digit code), or hold your iPhone near the accessory.
  3. If the device was already paired to another Matter app, tap More options and select it.
  4. When asked to add the accessory to your network, tap Allow. Assign a room and a name (Siri uses this name), then tap Continue and Done.
  5. Create a People Arrive automation that opens the garage, and a People Leave automation that closes it (Home app > Add (+) > Add Automation).

Tesla tie-ins

  • Tesla's own Smart Garage/HomeLink can open the door from the car "if available" on your vehicle, while the Home app handles everything else.
  • Schedule charging and preconditioning in the Tesla app (Schedule, app v4.34.5+).

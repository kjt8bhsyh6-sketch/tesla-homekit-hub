8 Tesla automations for Apple households

The Tesla iPhone app exposes actions to Siri and Apple Shortcuts (Tesla app 4.24+: lock/unlock, precondition, set temperature, defrost, seat heaters, vent or close windows, open frunk, open/close rear trunk and charge port, start/stop charging, set charge limit, Sentry and Dog Mode, and more). With iOS 18 you can also add Tesla controls (lock, climate, charge port, frunk, flash, honk) to Control Center. Action names can change between app versions, so check the Shortcuts app's Tesla section for what your version offers.

1. "Hey Siri, warm up the car"

Make a Shortcut using the Tesla app's climate action (turn climate on, optionally set temperature) and name it something easy to say. Run it by voice with Siri on your iPhone or Apple Watch.

2. Scheduled preconditioning

In the Tesla app, use Schedule to set a departure time so the car preconditions the cabin (and battery, when plugged in) before you leave. Precondition while plugged in to save range.

Home charging: Tesla Wall Connector on Amazon

3. Weather-triggered pre-heat

A Shortcuts personal automation at a set time can check the Weather action and run the Tesla climate action only if it's below a temperature you pick. Personal automations at a time of day can run without confirmation.

4. HomeKit garage on arrival

Tesla's built-in HomeLink requires compatible hardware/option; a HomeKit garage controller (like Meross MSG100 in our gear list) is an alternative. Add a Home app automation "When I arrive home" to open the garage, or tap it from Control Center. Apple requires confirmation on iPhone for security accessories like garage doors.

Opener used here: Meross garage opener on Amazon

5. "Leaving home" scene

A Home app automation when the last person leaves can turn off lights, set the thermostat to away, and lock doors. Pair it with a Shortcut that locks the car and closes the garage.

Needs a home hub: HomePod mini on Amazon

6. Lights on when you pull in at night

Home app "When I arrive" automation, limited to after sunset, turns on porch and garage lights.

Lights: Hue starter kit on Amazon · Sensor: Eve Motion on Amazon

7. Charging-friendly plug and outlet reminders

Use a Shortcuts automation when your iPhone disconnects from CarPlay or Bluetooth "Tesla" to remind yourself to plug in, or to show the car's status from the Tesla app's widget. (Avoid putting the car's charger on a smart plug: EV chargers draw far more than plugs are rated for.)

8. Open the frunk or trunk by voice

Add the Tesla app's trunk/frunk actions to Shortcuts and trigger them with Siri when your hands are full. Phone Key and Apple Watch still handle unlocking as you approach.
